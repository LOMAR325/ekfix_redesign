Common problems we fix

Six microwave faults
we see most often.

Every diagnostic includes a plain-language explanation of what's actually wrong — before we touch a part.

01

Not heating

A faulty magnetron or a bad high-voltage diode — the two most common failure points.

02

Turntable not turning

A worn motor or a broken coupler underneath the tray.

03

Sparking inside

A damaged waveguide cover — needs prompt replacement, don't keep running it.

04

Door won't latch or unit won't start

A door switch failure — this is a safety interlock, not a minor issue.

05

Display or buttons unresponsive

A control board fault behind the touchpad.

06

Loud humming or buzzing

A failing transformer or capacitor — we test before replacing the expensive part.

FAQ

Microwave repair,
answered honestly.

How quickly can you repair my microwave?

In most cases we can get a technician out same-day or within 24-48 hours. The repair itself usually takes place in a single visit once the issue is diagnosed and parts are on hand.

What if my microwave is beyond repair?

If a repair isn't cost-effective — for example a failed magnetron on a builder-grade unit — we'll tell you plainly and walk through your options instead of running up a bill on a lost cause.

What guarantee comes with the repair?

Every microwave repair carries a warranty on both the part and the labor. If the same issue comes back, we come back.

Do I need to bring the microwave anywhere?

No — microwave repairs are done on-site. We bring diagnostic equipment and common parts on the truck.

How much does microwave repair cost?

Diagnostics are $75, fully waived if you go ahead with the repair. The exact repair cost depends on the part needed and the brand, and we give you that number before any work begins.
