Common problems we fix

Six dryer faults
we see most often.

Every diagnostic includes a plain-language explanation of what's actually wrong — before we touch a part.

01

Not heating

A failed heating element on electric models, or an igniter issue on gas dryers. We test the heat source directly.

02

Takes multiple cycles to dry

Almost always a clogged lint vent or a failing moisture sensor — a real fire-risk issue we take seriously.

03

Won't tumble

A broken drive belt or a failing motor. We check both before replacing parts.

04

Loud thumping or squeaking

Worn drum rollers, glides, or bearings — the usual suspects for a noisy drum.

05

Won't start

Door switch, thermal fuse, or control board fault. We isolate which one is actually failing.

06

Overheats or shuts off mid-cycle

Restricted airflow or a failing thermostat. We check the full vent path, not just the machine.

FAQ

Dryer repair,
answered honestly.

How quickly can you repair my dryer?

In most cases we can get a technician out same-day or within 24-48 hours. The repair itself usually takes place in a single visit once the issue is diagnosed and parts are on hand.

What if my dryer is beyond repair?

If a repair isn't cost-effective — for example a scorched motor from a long-neglected clogged vent — we'll tell you plainly and walk through your options instead of running up a bill on a lost cause.

What guarantee comes with the repair?

Every dryer repair carries a warranty on both the part and the labor. If the same issue comes back, we come back.

Do I need to bring the dryer anywhere?

No — dryer repairs are done on-site. We bring diagnostic equipment and common parts on the truck.

How much does dryer repair cost?

Diagnostics are $75, fully waived if you go ahead with the repair. The exact repair cost depends on the part needed and the brand, and we give you that number before any work begins.
