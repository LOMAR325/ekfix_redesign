Common problems we fix

Six washer faults
we see most often.

Every diagnostic includes a plain-language explanation of what's actually wrong — before we touch a part.

01

Won't spin

A worn drive belt, failed motor coupling, or a stuck lid/door switch. We check the parts that actually transfer power to the drum.

02

Leaking water

Usually a worn door seal, a damaged hose, or a cracked tub. We find the exact source before replacing anything.

03

Won't drain

A clogged drain pump or kinked hose. We clear the blockage and test the pump under a real load.

04

Excessive vibration

Worn suspension springs, an unbalanced load sensor, or unlevel feet. We stabilize the machine properly, not just re-level it.

05

Won't start

Door lock, control board, or power supply fault. We troubleshoot the electrical side safely.

06

Foul odor or mold

Detergent buildup and drainage issues causing residue in the drum and gasket. We clean it out and adjust habits to stop it coming back.

FAQ

Washer repair,
answered honestly.

How quickly can you repair my washer?

In most cases we can get a technician out same-day or within 24-48 hours. The repair itself usually takes place in a single visit once the issue is diagnosed and parts are on hand.

What if my washer is beyond repair?

If a repair isn't cost-effective — for example a cracked tub on an older top-loader — we'll tell you plainly and walk through your options instead of running up a bill on a lost cause.

What guarantee comes with the repair?

Every washer repair carries a warranty on both the part and the labor. If the same issue comes back, we come back.

Do I need to bring the washer anywhere?

No — washer repairs are done on-site. We bring diagnostic equipment and common parts on the truck.

How much does washer repair cost?

Diagnostics are $75, fully waived if you go ahead with the repair. The exact repair cost depends on the part needed and the brand, and we give you that number before any work begins.
