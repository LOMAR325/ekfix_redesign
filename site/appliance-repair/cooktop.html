Common problems we fix

Six cooktop faults
we see most often.

Every diagnostic includes a plain-language explanation of what's actually wrong — before we touch a part.

01

Burner won't heat

An element failure on electric models, or an igniter/valve issue on gas.

02

Induction not detecting pans

A sensor calibration or control board issue specific to induction models.

03

Uneven heating

A worn element or a faulty control switch on that zone.

04

Touch controls unresponsive

The glass-top control panel or its board has failed — we test both.

05

Irregular gas flame

Clogged burner ports or a regulator issue affecting flame shape.

06

Cooktop won't power on

A wiring or control board fault, safely diagnosed before anything gets replaced.

FAQ

Cooktop repair,
answered honestly.

How quickly can you repair my cooktop?

In most cases we can get a technician out same-day or within 24-48 hours. The repair itself usually takes place in a single visit once the issue is diagnosed and parts are on hand.

What if my cooktop is beyond repair?

If a repair isn't cost-effective — for example a cracked induction glass surface — we'll tell you plainly and walk through your options instead of running up a bill on a lost cause.

What guarantee comes with the repair?

Every cooktop repair carries a warranty on both the part and the labor. If the same issue comes back, we come back.

Do I need to bring the cooktop anywhere?

No — cooktop repairs are done on-site. We bring diagnostic equipment and common parts on the truck.

How much does cooktop repair cost?

Diagnostics are $75, fully waived if you go ahead with the repair. The exact repair cost depends on the part needed and the brand, and we give you that number before any work begins.
